## Step 1 — Google Search Console Instant Indexing
*(Do this immediately once your domain is live)*

1. Visit [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Click **Add Property** -> Enter your domain or URL prefix: `https://codetyper.dev`.
3. Verify ownership (via DNS TXT record or HTML tag).
4. Go to **Sitemaps** on the left menu:
   - Enter `sitemap.xml` in the input field and click **Submit**.
   - Status should say **Success** within a few minutes.
5. In the top search bar, paste `https://codetyper.dev/` and press Enter.
6. Click the button: **"Request Indexing"**.
   - This bypasses standard crawl queues and requests Googlebot to crawl and index your site in 24–48 hours.
